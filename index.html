Questions

What sellers ask us first.

Do I have to make repairs or clean out the house?

No. We buy houses as-is. You can leave behind anything you do not want to take with you.

How do you decide what to offer?

We look at what similar houses nearby have sold for, then subtract the repairs the house needs and our costs to buy, hold and resell it. We are happy to walk you through the numbers.

Are there fees or commissions?

There is no agent commission, because you are selling directly to us. Any costs on your side are written into the offer before you sign, so there are no surprises at closing.

How fast can you close?

We pay cash, so there is no lender to wait on. Closing can usually happen within a few weeks once the title work is done. If you need longer, we close when you are ready.

Am I obligated if I ask for an offer?

No. The offer is free, and you can turn it down for any reason.

What situations do you buy in?

Inherited houses, rentals with tenants, houses behind on payments, houses with liens or code violations, and houses that simply need more work than the owner wants to do. If you are unsure, ask us.
